2. Privacy Policy

2.1 What We Collect

Hookly is designed with privacy by design. Here is what we collect:

  • Nothing by default. No account required, no email, no tracking pixels on the Free tier.
  • Local storage only: Your API keys (if you bring your own OpenAI/Groq), your scheduled posts, and your UI preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device.
  • Anonymous usage data (Pro only): Number of generations, feature usage, to improve the product. No personal data.

2.2 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email, or contact info (unless you voluntarily provide it)
  • ❌ Your API keys for OpenAI/Groq (those stay in your browser)
  • ❌ Your generated content (we don't log it)
  • ❌ Your browsing data or third-party trackers

2.3 How AI Providers Handle Your Data

When you use your own OpenAI or Groq API key, your prompts (niche, topic, tone) are sent directly from your browser to those providers. Their privacy policies apply:

By default, OpenAI and Groq do not use API requests to train their models. We recommend reviewing their policies for full details.
